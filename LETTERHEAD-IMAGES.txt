LETTERHEAD SECTION
No letterhead image was included in the uploads for this update, so the section is ready and currently displays an upload note.

To add letterhead designs later:
1. Create assets/letterheads/ folder.
2. Copy your letterhead image files into that folder.
3. Add image cards to the Letterhead Designs section in index.html using the same na-work-card + na-gallery-trigger pattern used in the other categories.

All newly uploaded visiting card, flyer and poster images are already included in assets/ and wired to the click-to-open popup.
